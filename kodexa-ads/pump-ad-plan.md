## 4. Ad level (step by step)
1. **Create ad → Manual upload → Video.** **Untick "Multi-advertiser ads".**
2. **Media:**
   - Upload **`ad-pump-4x5.mp4`**.
   - In **Edit placements / crop**, use **Replace** on the **Vertical 9:16** slot with **`ad-pump-9x16-safe.mp4`**. Don't crop.
   - Keep the others as Original.
3. **Thumbnail:** the frame at ~1 s (title + dashboard).
4. **Image generation:** none. **Enhancements:** turn **everything off** (video touch-ups, text improvements, essential enhancements). Ignore the "Apply / +points" nags.
5. **Text:** add the 3 primary texts, headline, description and CTA below.
6. **Preview:** Feeds should show the 4:5 (monitor + chips); Reels/Stories the 9:16. Trust the per-placement list over the small preview.
7. **Publish.**

## 7. Reading results (check after 3 days)
| Metric | Good | If bad |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per WhatsApp conversation | under Rs 250 (niche B2B audience) | over Rs 500: try primary text 2 first, or widen to all of Pakistan |
| 3-second views ÷ impressions | above 25% | below 15%: change the thumbnail/first frame |
| Conversations that share city + nozzles | 30%+ | low: make the greeting questions simpler |

- Pause if it spends **Rs 2,000 with zero conversations**.
- If it works: duplicate the ad set and raise the budget by ~20% every 2 days.
- Keep a simple sheet of leads: name, city, nozzles, CNG yes/no, demo sent, follow-up date.
