## Post 3: Question post (text only, no image)

**How to post:**
1. Create post and type the text.
2. Tap the **colour background (Aa)** button to put it on a coloured card. Dark or gold looks best.
3. If the text is too long for the coloured card, post it as plain text.

**Short version (fits a coloured background):**
```
Aapka business abhi orders kaise leta hai? 🤔
1️⃣ WhatsApp  2️⃣ Daraz  3️⃣ Apni website  4️⃣ Register/copy
Number comment karein 👇
```

**After people comment**, reply to each one, for example:
- **1 (WhatsApp):** "WhatsApp pe orders sambhalna mushkil hota hai na? Ek simple store se orders khud aate hain 🙂"
- **2 (Daraz):** "Daraz commission kaafi leta hai. Apni website pe margin poora aapka."
- **3 (Website):** "Zabardast! Speed check karni ho to link bhejein, free audit kar dete hain."
- **4 (Register):** "Hisaab ke liye hum offline software bhi banate hain, bina internet ke 👍"

### Alternative: Poll (Create post → ⋯ → Poll)
- **Question:** "Aapke business ke liye kya behtar hai?"
- **Options:** "Apna online store" / "Marketplace (bohat se sellers)"
